
🎬 Entertainment Content Strategy & Profitability Analysis
📌 Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end data analysis of a massive movie production dataset (7,663 movies). The goal of this analysis is to identify major areas of revenue leakage, understand audience engagement, and provide actionable, data-driven recommendations to optimize budget allocation and maximize Return on Investment (ROI).

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
SQL: Used for data cleaning, complex metric calculations (e.g., custom "User Ghosting Rate"), and aggregating large datasets.
Power BI: Used to build interactive and dynamic dashboards to visualize financial risk and strategic opportunities.

🚨 The Business Problems
Despite a massive total investment in content, the overall portfolio is hiding massive operational inefficiencies. This project solves three core issues:
- High Financial Risk: Almost 1 out of every 2 movies loses money (48% failure rate). A business cannot sustainably invest in content if half of its projects fail financially.
- Poor Audience Engagement: Low-quality content struggles to attract and retain audience attention. Low-rated movies receive incredibly low audience interaction (averaging only 22.6k votes compared to 511k for hits).
- Budget Allocation Inefficiency: Resources and budgets are not being allocated optimally, with money being poured into genres and budget tiers that have a high probability of failing.

3. Profit Optimization Strategy (The Solution)
Identifies exactly where the company should invest moving forward to guarantee high ROI and high audience engagement.

The Budget Trap: "Medium Budget" movies ($10M-$50M) are the riskiest. The business should either produce Low Budget movies (<$10M) for massive ROI (354%), or Blockbusters (>$100M) for guaranteed commercial success.

Content Sweet Spot: Action movies drive the highest profit, while Mystery drives the highest engagement. Long runtimes (>120 minutes) consistently outperform shorter formats.

💡 Strategic Recommendations
To optimize the content pipeline and eliminate revenue leakage, the business should:
Shift Budget Tiers: Stop funding "Medium Budget" projects. Reallocate funds to low-budget/high-ROI projects or safe-bet blockbusters.
Pivot Genre Focus: Reduce investments in high-loss genres like Thriller and Drama. Increase production of Action and Mystery films.
Standardize Runtimes: Aim for a runtime of over 120 minutes, as the data shows audiences prefer and engage more with long-form storytelling.
